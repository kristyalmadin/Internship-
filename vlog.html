THIS IS MY STORY

Kristianne is a fresh senior high school graduate from the STEM strand with aspirations of pursuing a degree in Medical Technology. She is currently undergoing and internship at the Department of Agriculture, where she is assigned at the ICT unit. Although she is new to the field of information and communication technology, she remains eager to learn, adapt, and grow. She tries her best to approach every task with curiosity and determination, and she is genuinely enjoying her experience as she expands her skills beyond her academic background.

KRISTY'S LIFE IN COLORS

“Kristy's Life in Colors” is a glimpse into the moments that quietly shape who I am. It is not just about big events or perfectly planned days, but the small, in between memories that often go unnoticed. From spontaneous laughter with friends to slow, quiet nights on my own, each clip holds a feeling that reflects a part of me. These are the moments that feel the most real, the ones I carry with me as I grow and change.

Each scene carries its own color and mood, sometimes bright and full of energy, sometimes soft and calm. Together, they create a life that feels honest and unfiltered. This vlog is not about perfection, but about capturing what is real and meaningful. It is my story as it unfolds, one moment at a time, held together by colors that feel like home.
